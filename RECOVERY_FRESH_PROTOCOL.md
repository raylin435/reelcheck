# Frozen fresh transcript-component evaluation

Declared before any generation on these 20 claims. Use the existing recovery prompts, models, parameters, budgets, validators and scoring without tuning. The development gate has two accepted target recoveries versus failed ordinary retries; these are operational wins, not proof of semantic superiority.

Twenty AI-paraphrased factual claims come from seven publisher video transcript pages: four NPS and three NOAA. Four conservative creator/series groups are retained (Glacier, Zion, NOAA Science at Sea, NOAA Ocean Today); there are only two publisher institutions. Seven videos do not constitute 20 independent samples. These claims and videos are fresh to this experiment; institutional/source overlap means this is not a strict publisher-held-out benchmark. Selection used readable publisher transcripts, varied mechanisms and qualifiers, not model outcomes or constructed falsehoods. No truth-label balance is promised. No candidate receives provenance, sources, labels or judge feedback.

This is a retrieval/verdict component evaluation. Audio, frames, transcript alignment and native short-form/portrait admission are NOT verified. Four NPS durations and the mapping video's approximate two-minute duration are publisher stated; the two Ocean Today durations are unverified and downloadable formats are landscape. Exclude the Chestnut Oak page because the publisher identifies it as audio. No video assets are redistributed. Claim provenance is not automatically independent factual evidence; candidates must find and read their own sources.

Freeze data/recovery_fresh_claims.jsonl and four deterministic round-robin five-case shards, configuration and relevant source hashes before inference. Shards allow four independent runner processes; calls within each remain sequential and all treatment settings are identical. Pairing remains within a case. Four shards each reserve at most $2 candidate inference and $3 assessment ($8/$12 total ceilings, not expected spending); web pricing remains unknown. A provider transport/access failure terminates that attempt and stops its advance pass; never retry that attempt. Continue unrelated attempts only. No changes or additional samples after seeing final outcomes.

Score all 20 cases and all three arms using the frozen consensus rule. Preserve unsuccessful/unresolved cases in denominators. Report shared-draft billing once, arm deployment attribution separately, group counts, abstention and decisive coverage. A judge truncation is unresolved, not rejection or silent regrading. Analyze execution-driven wins separately from pairs where both cheap arms completed. Describe evaluator concerns without overwriting frozen scores. Do not claim statistical significance, human accuracy, social-video accuracy, frontier equivalence, or total cost/latency superiority from this study.
